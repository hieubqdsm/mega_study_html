    Match the quality level performance of the Built-In Render Pipeline in URP

    After you use the Rendering Settings section in the Render Pipeline Converter, Unity creates the Universal Render Pipeline (URP) asset and Universal Renderer asset, then converts the settings from the Built-In Render Pipeline project to the corresponding settings in the URP assets.

    Your existing quality levels might provide a different level of performance. You need to manually update the quality levels in your URP project to match your quality levels in the Built-In Render Pipeline.

    Note: In URP, many quality level settings have moved from the Project Settings window to the URP asset. For more information on where to find these settings in URP projects, refer to Built-In Render Pipeline quality settings in URP reference.

    Quality levels mapping

    This section provides the recommended URP graphics Quality Level settings for Low and High quality level values. These settings approximately match the performance of the equivalent Low and High default presets in the Built-In Render Pipeline.
